1. Problem Statement & Bottlenecks

Section 01/03

In rural catchment sectors across Northern India, continuous monitoring of aquatic health remains critically hampered by physical sampling protocols. Currently, surface water specimens from canal inputs and non-point agricultural runoff require manual collection, refrigeration, and freight forwarding to accredited state laboratories in district centers.

Latency Lag
7 – 14 Days
Average duration between field sample extraction and validated lab report delivery.
High Capital Barrier
₹35,000+
Per-probe cost for imported optical spectrometry immersion heads.
Data Void
0 Continuous
Real-time telemetric streams from tertiary rural canal junctions.

By the time heavy loads of non-point synthetic chemical fertilizers (specifically Nitrates and active organophosphates) or illicit peripheral industrial effluents are identified, downstream agro-wells and rural drinking filtration nodes have already absorbed contaminated influxes. There is an urgent, national-scale imperative for university teams and collegiate engineering laboratories to pioneer ultra-low-cost, distributed edge-spectroscopic nodes capable of continuous in-situ detection.

Field Telemetry Architecture Target SPEC // ARCH-2026
Field Trial Benchmark Prototype

Continuous optical absorption spectrum monitoring unit calibrated against chemical laboratory reagents.

2. Concrete Functional Expectations

Section 02/03

Submissions will be programmatically and empirically benchmarked on the following 4 engineering parameters. Theoretical designs without functional hardware verification cannot qualify for grand finale stages.

Bill of Materials (BOM) < ₹4,500

Affordable Unit Cost Target

Complete hardware component cost (spectroscopic emitter/detector array, edge processor, power regulation, IP67 housing) cannot exceed ₹4,500 (~$55 USD) at 1,000-unit assembly scale.

Metric: Audited BOM spreadsheet verification
Chemical Accuracy PPM Precision

Nitrate & Fluoride Limits

Demonstrated limit of detection (LOD) for Nitrate ions (NO3⁻) below 10 mg/L and Fluoride below 1.5 mg/L in turbidity conditions ranging from 5 to 50 NTU without physical chemical pre-filtration.

Metric: Lab blind standard comparison test
Telemetry & Low-Power LoRaWAN / NB-IoT

Off-Grid Transmission

Autonomous payload delivery over unlicensed LoRaWAN bands (IN865) or NB-IoT networks with sleep power current consumption strictly under 25µA between reading cycles.

Metric: Current shunt power-draw profiling
Open Engineering Reproducible

Firmware & CAD Openness

Submission must link a public or evaluator-accessible Git repository containing PlatformIO / CMake firmware build instructions, schematic CAD (KiCad), and STEP 3D enclosure files.

Metric: Evaluator automated build script pass
